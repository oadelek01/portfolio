SHARPAI

An AI-driven platform that promotes thoughtful, responsible sports betting with personalized, data-informed insights.

REACTNODE.JSEXPRESS MONGODBPYTHONOPENAI GPT NLPJWTSPORTSDATA.IO API

WHAT IT DOES

Users submit a betslip — either as a form or as a photo of a physical slip — and SharpAI returns a full analysis: each team's last five games, injury reports, projections, a recommended bet amount and risk level, and an unbiased conclusion. A chat assistant then answers follow-up questions against that analysis.
